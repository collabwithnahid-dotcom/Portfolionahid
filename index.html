About Me

Nahid Hasan

I'm Nahid Hasan, a video editor who turns raw footage into videos people actually finish watching. I edit short-form content, long-form YouTube videos, real estate tours and UGC ads, so whatever you're making, I know how to give it the right pace and polish.

I handle the whole edit: cuts, color grading, sound, captions and motion. You send the clips, I send back something ready to post.

I care about three things: a strong hook, clean pacing and delivery you can count on. If you want your content to look sharper and keep people watching, let's talk.
